Benchmarks

Last measured: 23 to 25 August 2026

Every Ulpia number here comes from a RESULTS file in the open repository, with the command, commit, machine and date beside it, so a clone reruns it. Every competitor number is that vendor's own published claim, linked at its source.

Latency

Warm route, 1000 samples over 50 questions: p50 0.68 ms, p95 1.16 ms, the whole deterministic pipeline in process, no model, no network. Cold start, opening the fleet plus the first question: 136.4 ms. Commit 35322d2, 2026-08-23, machine and command in benchmarks/latency/RESULTS.md.

Their figures are server-side under their own harnesses, and a caller adds the network; ours is a local process with none to add. The comparison is of mechanisms, not machines: Zep's fastest self-reported retrieval is roughly 130 times our warm p95, and mem0's published search p50 is roughly 220 times our p50. What their price buys is automatic ingestion at scale, which Ulpia deliberately lacks.

Abstention

50 questions authored blind, 30 of them out of scope. The deterministic layer alone, no model, no network, left 28 of the 30 unanswered: silence, not a least-wrong document. The two misses are the set's named medical baits, and the answer layer above refused both, 2 of 2, a sample of two and labelled so. Matrix and provenance in benchmarks/abstention/RESULTS.md, commit 35322d2, 2026-08-23.

On LongMemEval-S, 28 of 30 abstention questions were correct under the hardest reading mode. The benchmark's own paper reports abstention as the ability long-memory systems fail hardest; no competitor's published results quote an abstention split at all.

LongMemEval-S

500 questions against long chat histories. Ulpia scores 61 percent, 305 of 500, with the reading mode declared per question nature; the all-default first run scored 49 percent and stays published. Judged locally by claude-haiku, not the official protocol, official re-judging pending; ingestion is mechanical keys, the weakest honest kind, so the number is a floor. Per-ability breakdown in benchmarks/longmemeval/RESULTS.md.

Each vendor number is self-judged under its own configuration with its own answering model, several of them dispute each other's methodology in public, and none is comparable to the others or to ours. Ours is the only one reproducible from a clone of a public repository.

What this page refuses

Ingestion tuned to the benchmark, and private harnesses: a number we cannot hand you the command for does not appear here. LongMemEval-V2 scores accuracy against latency as one frontier, the terrain a millisecond retrieval path is built for; not measured yet, so no number.
