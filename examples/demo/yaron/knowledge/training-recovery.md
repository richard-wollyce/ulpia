**Exists to:** Why rest days are part of the program, textbook version

Adaptation happens between sessions, not during them. Textbook guidance: a muscle
group recovers in 48 to 72 hours, a deload week every four to eight weeks keeps
progression honest, and persistent soreness plus falling performance is the signal to
rest, not to push.
