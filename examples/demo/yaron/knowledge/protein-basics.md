**Exists to:** The textbook protein ranges and how to spread them across meals

Textbook range for active adults: 1.6 to 2.2 g of protein per kilogram of body weight
per day, spread across three to five meals of 25 to 40 g each, because synthesis
responds per meal rather than per day. These are population numbers from published
guidelines, not a prescription.
