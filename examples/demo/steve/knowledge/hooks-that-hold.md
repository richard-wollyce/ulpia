**Exists to:** Why openings decide retention, and the three hook shapes that hold

A viewer decides in the first seconds whether the video owes them anything. Three
openings hold: the unresolved question, the result shown before the method, and the
contradiction of something the audience believes. A hook that promises what the video
does not deliver buys one view and sells the channel.
